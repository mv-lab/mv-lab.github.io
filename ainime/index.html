概要 About

Animation is no longer only hand-drawn anime. Models can now animate a still photo or synthesize stylized sequences — yet we still lack shared answers: How do we assess natural movement versus generated movement? What benchmarks capture temporal coherence, identity, and style? How do we control motion and animation with diffusion models and world models?

AINIME is, to our knowledge, the first workshop on this dual agenda: traditional anime/manga and natural-video animation. Creators need the tools; researchers need the venue. We bring generation, post-production, restoration, and quality evaluation onto one WACV stage — with a flagship Anime Restoration Challenge on Re-Anime600.

Built on the Re-Anime600 dataset and the AIGENS workshop at CVPR 2026.

募集 Call for Papers

We cover the whole production pipeline — from generation and post-processing to restoration and quality evaluation of natural and anime-style videos.

  • Image-/text-to-video animation — natural and stylized
  • Motion control — diffusion models and world models
  • Natural movement vs. animation — new metrics and studies
  • Benchmarks & datasets — temporal quality, identity, style
  • Anime post-production — restoration, upscaling, colorization
  • Responsible creative AI — authorship, consent, production tools

Regular papers, 5–8 pages (excl. references), double-blind review via OpenReview. The track is archival — accepted papers appear in the WACV 2027 workshop proceedings / IEEE Xplore.
